# Project idea: Dog HQ — MVP research

- Date: 2026-06-25
- Category: project idea / micro-SaaS validation
- Short verdict: build a narrow MVP
- Difficulty: medium — simple local-first MVP is easy, real multi-user sharing and reminders add complexity
- Potential monetisation: freemium, €4.99/month for caregiver sharing, PDF exports, multi-dog, reminders, behavior logs

## Problem

Dog owners track many small but important signals: feeding, water, walks, poop, medication, vaccines, weight, mood, anxiety, vet visits, expenses, and caregiver instructions. The market is fragmented: one app for GPS, another for walking services, another for training, another for vet records. Owners often fall back to Apple Notes, WhatsApp, SMS, Google Docs, or a whiteboard.

## Community / market signal

Research buckets found repeated themes:

- Dog owners want medication and vaccine reminders, health records, daily care logs, and caregiver handoff.
- GPS/activity apps such as Whistle, Fi, and Tractive often require hardware.
- Training apps such as Dogo and Woofz focus on lessons, not household coordination.
- Vet apps such as PetDesk and Pawprint focus on medical records/appointments, often US-centric or tied to clinic adoption.
- The most underserved wedge is shared care: partner, family, petsitter, dog walker, and vet/behaviorist handoff.

## Competitor analysis

### PetDesk / Pawprint / Bark&

Strengths: health records, reminders, medical timeline. Weaknesses: not built around daily household care, caregiver roles, quick handoff, or European/local language positioning.

### Whistle / Fi / Tractive

Strengths: GPS, activity, hardware signal. Weaknesses: requires collar/device, subscription, not a household operating system.

### Dogo / Woofz / GoodPup

Strengths: training content and behavior guidance. Weaknesses: not focused on daily care, medication, vet exports, family coordination.

### Rover / Wag

Strengths: marketplace and sitter/walker booking. Weaknesses: useful mainly when using their marketplace; not a neutral dog passport / family care system.

## Opportunity map

The best wedge is not “all-in-one pet app”. It is “Dog HQ for shared care”: a daily command center that answers:

- Has the dog eaten?
- Was there a walk?
- Did they poop? Was it normal?
- Was medication given?
- What should the sitter know?
- What should I tell the vet/behaviorist?

## MVP recommendation

Build a local-first single-page MVP with:

1. Landing page and positioning.
2. Dog profile.
3. Daily care log: walk, food, water, poop, medication, mood, training.
4. Reminders: vaccine, medication, vet, grooming.
5. Dog Passport: copyable summary for caregiver/vet.
6. LocalStorage persistence.

## MVP technical shape

- React + TypeScript + Vite.
- No backend in v0.
- LocalStorage for dog profile, logs, reminders.
- Responsive UI, warm premium style.
- Future path: PWA, user accounts, share links, PDF export, push notifications.

## Monetisation options

- Free: one dog, basic local logs.
- Premium: caregiver sharing, PDF exports, reminders, multi-dog, behavior analytics.
- Partner channels: vets, behaviorists, groomers, dog sitters.

## Differentiation

- Shared-care angle instead of generic “pet app”.
- Dog Passport for handoff.
- Polish/european positioning where competition is weaker.
- Behavior and anxiety notes as structured data for behaviorists.

## Go-to-market

- Polish FB dog groups and dog sitter communities.
- Short TikTok/IG reels: “what to send a petsitter in 10 seconds”.
- SEO in Polish around “aplikacja dla właściciela psa”, “dziennik zdrowia psa”, “przypomnienie o szczepieniu psa”.
- Vet/behaviorist lead magnet: printable PDF dog history.

## Ways to improve after MVP

- Real invitation link and roles.
- PDF export.
- Calendar + push reminders.
- Multi-dog support.
- Weight and behavior charts.
- Photos and attachments.
- PWA offline install.

## Final recommendation

Build/test. Avoid broad pet-platform scope. Validate with a narrow message: “Cozi / command center for your dog — daily care, medication, and sitter handoff.”
