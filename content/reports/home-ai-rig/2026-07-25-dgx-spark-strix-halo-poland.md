## AI Usefulness Notes

- **DGX Spark / Dell Pro Max / HP ZGX Nano**: 128GB unified memory (Grace Blackwell GB10). Best for local LLMs up to ~70B. NVIDIA CUDA/Linux support excellent. Cost ~23–27k PLN.
- **GMKtec EVO-X2 64GB**: 64GB shared RAM (Strix Halo). Good for up to ~30B models. ROCm support maturing. Compact. ~9.8k PLN is attractive entry.
- **GMKtec EVO-X2 128GB**: 128GB shared RAM. Theoretically runs 70B+ models. ROCm needed. ~16.5k PLN — very competitive vs DGX Spark for memory alone.
- **Flow Z13 32GB used**: Only 32GB — limited to ~13B models comfortably. Interesting as portable AI dev device but not a serious inference rig.
- **HP ZBook Ultra G1a 128GB**: Laptop form factor with 128GB unified. Premium price (~18.7k) but portable + Strix Halo + ROCm.
