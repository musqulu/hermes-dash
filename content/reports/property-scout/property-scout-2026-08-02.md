## Evidence notes

Otodom and SiedliskaOnline pages were accessible enough to extract price, location, land/building sizes and selected features from listing metadata/snippets. Legal/planning status is mostly unknown except Kłaczyna’s claimed MPZP; do not rely on seller wording without KW, map portal, MPZP/WZ and flood/protection checks.

## Seller/gmina checks before viewing

Ask for KW number, plot numbers, MPZP/WZ extract, road status/easement proof, building permit/use status for houses/outbuildings/ponds, utility connection documents, septic legality, roof age, damp/foundation issues, heating type and annual costs. For Ostrowice and any water-adjacent land, check flood maps, wetlands, Natura 2000/landscape park restrictions and whether any water access is legal or only nearby marketing copy.
