## Buyer-side view

This is a **maybe / needs diligence**, not a bargain to chase emotionally. The good part is that the listing gives unusually concrete planning data and usable services/access, and 7,600 m² is enough for a private home, garden, workshop, or small retreat concept. The weak part is price: ~66 PLN/m² is high for pure rural land, so the value depends heavily on MPZP, buildability over the full plot, and whether the road/utilities are genuinely low-friction.

## Due diligence questions

- Ask for the parcel number, KW number, and full MPZP excerpt/rysunek for the exact plot.
- Confirm whether the whole 7,600 m² is buildable or only part of it.
- Confirm road ownership: public road frontage vs private/easement, width, winter maintenance.
- Confirm actual utility connection distances/costs, especially water and current sewage/septic option.
- Ask whether any part is flood-prone, wet, protected, or constrained by the Nysa Szalona valley.
- Ask whether soil class I–III creates any conversion fees or building restrictions despite MPZP.

## Negotiation angle

Treat 500k as an anchor, not fair value. Push on the high PLN/m², current lack of sewerage, agricultural soil-class risk, and the need to verify how much of the parcel is actually usable/buildable. A serious offer should be conditional on KW/MPZP/access/utility documents.
