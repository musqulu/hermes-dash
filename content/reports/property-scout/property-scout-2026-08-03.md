## Buyer-side next questions

Ask each seller/agent for: KW number, plot numbers, MPZP/WZ extract, soil/forest classes, exact legal road access, utility connection status and distances, flood/wetland/protected-area constraints, and whether any easements, conservation restrictions, leases, or KOWR/agricultural limits apply. For houses, request roof/structure/damp/heating photos and renovation history before travelling.
