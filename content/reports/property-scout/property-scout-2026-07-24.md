## Evidence and buyer notes

Collected from live OLX search/category pages and direct listing pages. OLX pages were accessible enough to expose titles, prices, locations, land/building sizes and descriptions, though they also contained captcha markup, so all details should be re-confirmed by phone and documents.

Best buyer-side calls today are **Kolsk** and **Racław**: both have enough land and outbuildings to create real optionality, and both sit below 600k PLN. Before viewing, ask for KW number, plot IDs, MPZP/WZ status, land classes, building year, roof condition, damp/foundation issues, heating, septic/sewage, water source, electricity capacity, and whether access is public road or easement. For the pond property, also ask if the pond is legal/registered and whether the land is wet or flood-prone.

The **Lipa 9.5 ha** parcel is the most asymmetric land-only lead, but it can be a trap if it is pure agricultural/landscape land with no realistic building path. Call the gmina before the seller pitch: MPZP, protected-area constraints, road status, utilities distance, slope and whether any WZ/habitat route is plausible.
